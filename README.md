## Prerequisites

Barry runs on MacOS, but its supported versions are not privy to you - or me.

- Homebrew
- Homebrew node, major 26 (matches .node-version)
- pnpm major 10
- Orbstack

> ⚠️ Barry is omnipresent - wakes up when you do (see [launchd](docs/launchd.md) docs).
